01New Student Orientation

You enroll automatically when you participate. Your first three steps: check your student record with !profile, learn how tugrug and XP are earned, and try a classmate challenge when you are ready. You do not have to memorize the whole school on day one. More activities are always available through !help.

Useful commands: !help [topic]

02Competitions

Start with !challenge @student. Your classmate can !accept or !decline. Once accepted, each participant stakes 25 tugrug. The winner is paid 40 tugrug total and earns 5 XP; the loser earns 1 XP. That means the winner gains a net 15 tugrug, while the loser loses the 25-tugrug stake. Your stats and outfits can influence the result or the competition selected. You do not need to know the hidden selection calculations to play.

Useful commands: !challenge @student · !accept · !decline [reason]

03Tugrug & XP

Eligible chat messages award 5 tugrug, subject to a 60-second chat reward cooldown. Every five rewarded minutes of eligible presence awards 10 tugrug; every ten eligible minutes also awards 1 XP, with partial progress carrying across streams. Accepted challenge wins award 5 XP and losses award 1 XP. Check !tugrug for your balance and !profile for your overall progress. Not every message or minute qualifies for rewards. Employed students can also earn wages or Library XP by working shifts. See the After School annex for the shared activity cooldown.

Useful commands: !tugrug

04School Store

Use !shop to browse departments, !shopinfo NAME to check a product, and !buy NAME to purchase. Use !inventory to see what you own and !collection for permanently owned collectibles. Change owned equipment with !equip NAME and outfits with !wear NAME. Use !eat NAME or !drink NAME to consume food you already own; purchase food with !buy NAME first. Ms. Lauren's Specimen Pilot Program begins when you buy a Specimen Egg and use !hatch. Limited-time collectibles such as the Mortal Kombat Cap appear when their sale offer is active. Vehicles are now available through !shop vehicles; equip one with !drive NAME and view your garage with !garage.

Useful commands: !shop [category] · !shopinfo NAME · !buy NAME · !inventory · !equip NAME · !wear NAME · !eat NAME · !drink NAME · !hatch · !specimen

Need the full price list? Browse the complete School Store Catalog, including vehicles, prices and item descriptions.

05Student Records

!profile shows your overall student record: level, XP, attributes, wallet, and possessions. !stats gives a shorter attribute view, and !tugrug checks your balance. !reportcard focuses on your competition results and Principal’s List rank. !leaderboard shows schoolwide standings; !leaderboard Math Debate opens an example competition Honor Roll. !collection displays your collectibles. Career and racing records are available with !job, !resume, !paystub, !workboard, !racestats and !raceboard. See the After School annex.

Useful commands: !profile · !leaderboard [competition] · !collection · !reportcard

06Stats & Leveling

Every level gained awards one stat point. Distribute points among Athleticism, Intelligence, Creativity, and Rizz with !allocate STAT AMOUNT; for example, !allocate rizz 1. Use !stats to check your attributes. Equipment, specimens, and cafeteria food may provide additional bonuses. Different competitions favor different strengths.

Useful commands: !stats · !allocate STAT AMOUNT

07School Life

School life isn't just competitions. Use !study [topic] for an encouraging study reply, !skip [department] to try skipping a class (the school may catch you), and !nap / !wake to announce when you're away and back. They don't directly add tugrug, XP, or stats. School activities and spontaneous events may happen during a stream, and not every surprise is described here. Try !timecapsule to replay a random recording from the school archives for 50 tugrug. The school PA may be busy; unsuccessful requests aren't charged. Use !announce MESSAGE (up to 150 characters) for a moderated PA announcement. A successful broadcast costs 100 tugrug; rejected messages are not charged and a 3-minute schoolwide cooldown applies.

Useful commands: !timecapsule · !announce MESSAGE · !study [topic] · !skip [department] · !nap · !wake

08School Rules & Costs

Before accepting a challenge, know the cost: both players stake 25 tugrug; the winner receives 40 total. Some school activities and items have additional costs or cooldowns. Your school experience should stay fun for everyone: follow the stream’s moderation rules, avoid chat spam, and use !shopinfo NAME to check an item before buying. The handbook does not reveal every school surprise.

09Command Directory

Type any command in Twitch chat. For a quick explanation, type !help followed by the command name, such as !help hatch.

After-school commands: Find all employment, transportation, practice and racing commands in the After School command directory.

!help [topic]

📚 !help shows the Student Handbook menu. Add a topic (like !help compete) or command (like !help hatch) for a short explanation.

!profile

📋 !profile shows your current level and XP, attributes, tugrug, competition record, and equipped possessions.

!stats

📈 !stats provides a quick view of your four attributes and available stat points. See !help stats to learn how to improve them.

!tugrug

💰 !tugrug checks your current school-currency balance. You can earn more through eligible participation and competitions.

!allocate STAT AMOUNT

📈 !allocate STAT AMOUNT spends earned level-up points on an attribute. Example: !allocate rizz 1. You can't undo an allocation, so choose carefully.

!challenge @student

🏆 !challenge @student invites an eligible classmate to compete. If accepted, each stakes 25 tugrug; the winner gets 40 and 5 XP, while the loser gets 1 XP.

!accept

✅ !accept accepts a pending challenge directed at you. An accepted challenge stakes 25 tugrug from each participant.

!decline [reason]

🙅 !decline refuses a pending challenge. You can optionally add a short reason. A refusal doesn't start a competition.

!shop [category]

🛒 Browse departments including vehicles, outfits, equipment, cafeteria food, collectibles and Specimen Eggs. Try !shop vehicles.

!shopinfo NAME

🔎 !shopinfo NAME shows information about a school-store item before you buy it. Example: !shopinfo Specimen Egg.

!buy NAME

🛍️ !buy NAME purchases an available item with tugrug. Example: !buy Ramen. Use !shopinfo NAME to review its cost and description first.

!inventory

🎒 !inventory lists possessions, including equipment, outfits, food, eggs, and your currently equipped specimen. Use !garage for vehicles.

!equip NAME

🎒 !equip NAME switches your equipped item to equipment you already own. Example: !equip Kindle. Only one equipment item can be equipped at a time; switching is free outside active challenges.

!wear NAME

👕 !wear NAME switches to an outfit you already own. Example: !wear VCU Hello Kitty Shirt. Only one outfit can be worn at a time, and switching is free outside active challenges.

!eat NAME

🍜 Consume owned cafeteria food. Most foods give a temporary stat bonus for three completed challenges; Gong Cha Boba lasts two. Only one stat food buff can be active at a time.

!drink NAME

🥤 !drink NAME also consumes an owned cafeteria item. It works with the same food inventory and buff system as !eat NAME.

!hatch

🥚 !hatch opens an owned Specimen Egg and reveals your new specimen. You can have one specimen equipped at a time.

!specimen

🧪 !specimen shows your current specimen. Specimens are part of Ms. Lauren's Specimen Pilot Program.

!leaderboard [competition]

🏅 !leaderboard shows the Principal's List. Add a competition name (e.g., !leaderboard Math Debate) to view that event's Honor Roll.

!collection

🗃️ !collection displays your permanently owned collectibles, including limited-time items you've purchased. Collectibles are separate from equipment, outfits, and specimens.

!reportcard

📋 !reportcard shows your competition W–L record, win percentage, and Principal's List standing.

!timecapsule

📼 !timecapsule requests a random archived recording for 50 tugrug. If playback cannot start because the school PA is busy, no tugrug is charged.

!announce MESSAGE

📣 !announce MESSAGE submits a moderated school PA message (up to 150 characters). Successful broadcasts cost 100 tugrug; a 3-minute schoolwide cooldown applies. Rejected requests are not charged.

!study [topic]

📚 !study [topic] gets a school-themed study reply. Choose a subject or leave it blank; there is a 10-minute cooldown. No tugrug, XP, or stat bonus.

!skip [department]

🚪 !skip [department] tries sneaking out of class. Name a department or leave it random. You might get caught! A 60-second cooldown applies; no tugrug or XP.

!nap

💤 !nap tells classmates you are stepping away for a bit. Purely social; it does not directly earn tugrug or XP. Use !wake when you return.

!wake

☀️ !wake announces your return from a nap. This is a social check-in, not a way to claim extra watch-time rewards.

Administrative notice: Costs, cooldowns, eligibility, and moderation rules apply. Some parts of school life are meant to be discovered rather than catalogued.
