Attendance & switching: Practicing instead of working can count toward missed work opportunities while employed. Work resets attendance. Quitting or changing jobs costs three credited shifts at the old employer (not lifetime shifts); earned ranks are retained. Job changes are limited to once per stream and subject to a 24-hour cooldown. Switching blocks work until the next stream. Confirm requested changes using !confirmjob.

02Transportation

Browse all 13 purchasable vehicles in the Vehicles department. Buy with !buy NAME and equip separately with !drive NAME. Speed affects racing times; Convenience shortens the next shared after-school cooldown. Aura is a separate vehicle rating shown in the catalog.

Domino's employees can use !rentscooter for a company scooter during the current stream. The rental expires when you leave that job or the stream ends. The company Corolla is a career reward, not a purchase listing.

!shop vehicles · !shopinfo NAME · !buy NAME · !drive NAME · !garage

04Competition Proficiency

Practice a specific competition (not a department or stat). Each completed practice adds one practice to that event. Proficiency can improve your odds when competing in that same event; an opponent's proficiency also matters. It never guarantees victory.

05Parking Lot Racing

Race on foot or with a vehicle using !race. On foot has Speed 0. Classmates can join with !joinrace or !race. The lobby needs 2–4 racers; it begins with 30 seconds and may extend as racers join. Watch the overlay and wait for each chat prompt before entering your first answer.

↑!s

■!b

4 checkpoints · 12 seconds each

Correct and faster responses reduce penalties. Speed helps determine the base score.

25 tugrug winner prize

Official races require at least two racers with at least one correct answer each. Draws and exhibitions do not award a winner prize.

Finish times are scored game times, not real-world stopwatch times. Full standings are posted in chat. The school cooldown is five minutes and the participant cooldown is ten minutes. Racing requires the live visual server and available school event slot.

!racestats · !raceboard (career wins) · !raceboard Parking Lot (top five personal bests)

06Career Recognition & Benefits

Your first promotion at each employer awards a collectible. Final-rank benefits vary by employer. Employee of the Week recognizes the most completed shifts in a Monday–Sunday school week (Eastern Time); tied leaders can each earn a badge. Use !workboard and !collection to check progress and rewards.

Gong Cha employee drinks

Eligible students may claim one per stream with !boba or a flavor name, then consume it with !drink NAME. These are employee rewards, not purchasable shop items. They share the ordinary four-item cafeteria limit and one active stat-food buff.
